The don

A Londoner in Australia, with a drone and a plan

The Drone_Father is a one-man creative outfit: a Londoner who moved to Australia and started pointing a camera at the world from above. Glaciers, sand islands, Italian lakes, Greek and Croatian coastlines, city skylines after dark.

Every video is flown, shot and cut in-house. No stock footage, no shortcuts. Just places worth seeing, from an angle you don't usually get.
